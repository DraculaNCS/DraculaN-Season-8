# **Tournament Format**

## **Overview of Stages**

DraculaN Season 8 spans six days, featuring a Qualifying Group Stage and a Double Elimination Playoff bracket. With 24 teams signed up by the registration cutoff date, the format is structured as follows:

**Days 1 and 2, Group Stage:** All 24 teams compete in 8 round-robin groups of 3 teams each. Group winners advance directly to the Playoffs. The second- and third-place teams in each group play a BO3 decider for the remaining Playoff spot.

**Days 2 to 6, Playoffs:** A 16-team Double Elimination bracket, consisting of the 8 group winners and the 8 decider winners.

## **Group Stage (Days 1 and 2)**

### **Participants**

All 24 registered teams.

### **Group Formation**

Teams are divided into 8 groups of 3 using a seeded draw system. Seeding is based on a three-pot allocation, as described in the Seeding Determination section:

* **Pot 1:** Top 8 teams

* **Pot 2:** Next 8 teams

* **Pot 3:** Remaining 8 teams

Each group will contain exactly one team from each pot. The Group Stage draw will be posted on the official Discord channel, [https://discord.gg/rZ4r3d3ahx](https://discord.gg/rZ4r3d3ahx), as well as on our X account, [https://x.com/digital_crusade](https://x.com/digital_crusade).

### **Group Format**

Each team will play the other two teams in its group in BO1 matches. The second- and third-place teams in each group will face each other in a BO3 decider match.

### **Group Standings and Tiebreakers**

Group standings are determined by the number of BO1 match wins. Where teams are tied, the following criteria apply in order:

1. Head-to-head result, if exactly two teams are tied.
2. Overall round differential across the group's two BO1 matches for each tied team, including overtime rounds.
3. If the preceding criterion leaves exactly two teams tied, their head-to-head result.
4. Original tournament seed, with the higher seed placed first, if a three-way tie remains unresolved.

The original tournament seed is established before play starts and is not affected by subsequent VRS publications. No additional tiebreaker match is scheduled. The final group order determines advancement and the qualification match participants; it does not eliminate any team by itself.

### **Match Rules**

Overtime is enabled for all matches and draws are not possible. If a Best-of-1 map ends 12-12, overtime is played using MR3 with USD 10,000 start money until a winner is determined. Best-of-3 series follow the same overtime rules. Half-time breaks shall be 3 minutes.

### **Advancement**

The first-place team in each group will advance directly to the Playoffs, while the second- and third-place teams will face each other in a BO3 decider match. The winner of each decider match will advance to the Playoffs, while the loser will be eliminated.

A total of 16 teams will advance to the Double Elimination Playoffs.

If a group consists of only two teams, both teams advance to the Playoffs. Instead of the round-robin and decider matches, the two teams will play one BO3 match to determine their Playoff seeding. The winner is treated as the group winner and the loser as the second-place qualifier for Playoff seeding purposes.

### **Group Scheduling**

Group Stage matches are conducted in waves across available setups. Teams must be ready on the server at least 15 minutes before the scheduled start time. The detailed match schedule will be communicated to all participating teams in advance of the event.

## **Playoffs (Days 2 to 6)**

### **Participants**

16 teams in total:

* 8 Group Stage winners.

* 8 winners of the BO3 decider matches (or second-place qualifiers in groups of two teams).

### **Bracket Format**

Double Elimination Bracket. Teams are eliminated only after two match losses, except in the Grand Final, which has no bracket reset.

### **Match Format**

All Playoff matches, including the Grand Final, are Best-of-3. All maps use the MR12 format, with overtime (MR3, USD 10,000 start money) if required.

### **Opening Playoff Round Seeding**

The Opening Playoff Round follows fixed cross-group pairings, determined before the tournament begins. Each group winner faces the second-place qualifier from another group, as follows:

| Match | Group Winner | Second-Place Qualifier |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Match 1** | Group A | Group B |
| **Match 2** | Group C | Group D |
| **Match 3** | Group E | Group F |
| **Match 4** | Group G | Group H |
| **Match 5** | Group B | Group A |
| **Match 6** | Group D | Group C |
| **Match 7** | Group F | Group E |
| **Match 8** | Group H | Group G |

The second-place qualifier is the winner of the BO3 decider match or the loser of the BO3 seeding match in a two-team group.

The winners of Matches 1 and 2, Matches 3 and 4, Matches 5 and 6, and Matches 7 and 8 face each other respectively in the Upper Bracket Quarterfinals. These bracket positions remain fixed throughout the Playoffs. Playoff seeds for veto purposes are determined separately, as described in the Seeding Determination section.

Losers of the Opening Playoff Round drop into the Lower Bracket and remain in contention. Winners advance in the Upper Bracket.

### **Playoff Placements**

The Double Elimination bracket resolves final placements as follows: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, joint 5th to 6th, joint 7th to 8th, joint 9th to 12th and joint 13th to 16th. Teams eliminated in the Group Stage are placed jointly 17th to 24th. Tied placements are determined by elimination round, with no additional tiebreaker matches between teams sharing a placement.

## **Grand Final**

The Upper Bracket winner and the Lower Bracket winner face each other in a Best-of-3 Grand Final.

* No bracket reset.

* No map advantage.

* The Upper Bracket finalist is considered the higher seed by default.

This match is winner-take-all for the title of DraculaN Season 8 champion.

# **Seeding Determination**

## **Group Seeds**

All participating teams are seeded before the tournament begins into three pots, based on the official Global VRS rankings released by Valve in December 2026. Pot 1 contains seeds 1 to 8, Pot 2 contains seeds 9 to 16, and Pot 3 contains seeds 17 to 24. VRS-ranked teams are placed ahead of teams without a Global VRS ranking. Teams without a Global VRS ranking are ordered by their HLTV ranking. Teams without either a Global VRS ranking or an HLTV ranking are ordered by the average FACEIT Elo of their registered five players. Each group is initially drawn with one team from each pot.

There are no direct VRS invitations to this event. All registered teams begin in the Group Stage to earn their place in the Playoffs.

## **Playoff Seeds**

* **Seeds 1 to 8:** The eight Group Stage winners, ordered by their original tournament seed.

* **Seeds 9 to 16:** The eight winners of the BO3 decider matches (or second-place qualifiers in groups of two teams), ordered by their original tournament seed.

This seeding order is used for veto purposes in the Playoffs and does not alter the fixed Opening Playoff Round pairings. The Upper Bracket finalist has veto priority in the Grand Final.

In every match, in both the Group Stage and the Playoffs, the higher-seeded team has priority to choose either map veto order or starting side.

## **Best-of-1 Pick and Ban Process**

Used in all round-robin Group Stage matches.

* The higher seed chooses to be Team A or Team B.

* Team A removes 2 maps.

* Team B removes 3 maps.

* Team A removes 1 map.

* The remaining map is played.

* Team B chooses the starting side on the selected map.

## **Best-of-3 Pick and Ban Process**

Used in the Group Stage Decider Matches, seeding matches in groups of two teams, and all Playoff matches, including the Grand Final.

* The higher seed chooses to be Team A or Team B.

* Team A removes 1 map.

* Team B removes 1 map.

* Team A picks Map 1\.

* Team B chooses the starting side on Map 1\.

* Team B picks Map 2\.

* Team A chooses the starting side on Map 2\.

* Team B removes 1 map.

* Team A removes 1 map.

* The remaining map becomes Map 3, the decider.

* Team B chooses the starting side on Map 3\.

# **Administration**

**Veto Supervision:** The veto process is supervised by a tournament admin. It may be conducted on stage, through the server, or via an online veto tool. Teams must make their ban and pick decisions promptly, and a time limit of 1 to 2 minutes per decision may be enforced.

**Overtime Configuration:** If a map is tied 12-12 at the end of regulation, overtime is played to determine a winner. The server is configured for 6 maximum overtime rounds, 3 per half, and USD 10,000 for each overtime half. Teams do not switch sides from the end of regulation for the start of overtime; they continue on the side they finished on, then swap after 3 overtime rounds. Overtime repeats until a winner is decided.

**Match Server and Config:** Matches are hosted on provided tournament servers with a standard config. Teams have a brief window to set up their personal configurations before live play. It is each team's responsibility to verify their game settings and equipment on the server before the match goes live. Once both teams signal readiness, the match starts. If a team fails to raise any config or setting issue before the first round goes live, the match proceeds under the assumption that all is in order.

# **Pauses and Timeout Rules**

**Tactical Pauses:** Each team is allowed up to 3 tactical timeouts per map, with 1 additional timeout in overtime, each lasting 30 seconds. These pauses are for team discussion and strategy. Teams may use the in-game timeout command to initiate them. Multiple timeouts may be used in a row if needed. Once a team has exhausted its 3 timeouts in a map, no further tactical pauses are allowed for that map.

**Technical Pauses:** If a player experiences a technical issue such as a hardware malfunction, disconnect or server lag, they should immediately type "\!tech" in chat and pause the game if possible. Players must not abuse the pause function; only legitimate technical issues warrant a pause. During a technical pause, players, coaches and team staff must remain at their positions and may not discuss any game-related topics. The coach is not permitted to speak during a technical pause.

**Admin Pauses:** Tournament administrators can pause a match at any time from the server side if an issue is observed. Admin pauses can be used to resolve disputes, fix server problems or handle interference. Players must remain ready during admin pauses and follow any instructions given. During an admin pause, players, coaches and team staff must remain at their positions and may not discuss any game-related topics. The coach is not permitted to speak during an admin pause.

**Duration of Pauses:** Tactical timeouts are fixed at 30 seconds. Technical pauses last only as long as necessary to resolve the issue. If a team cannot fix a problem within a reasonable time, generally 10 minutes maximum, the admins may force the match to continue or, in extreme cases, issue a forfeit if the team cannot continue playing. Teams should have any backup equipment ready to swap in quickly to avoid long delays.

# **Match Interruptions and Recovery**

We aim for smooth matches, but in case of interruptions such as a server crash, power outage or network issue, the following rules apply to maintain competitive integrity.

**Server Crash or All Players Drop:** If the server crashes or all players disconnect simultaneously, the round in progress is nullified and will be replayed, started at the same score as before the round. The server will be restarted if needed, and an admin will use CS2's match recovery tools, if available, to restore the match to the last completed round. Managers, coaches and team staff are not permitted to intervene with players during this process, including the use of phones, streams or external information to recreate the round state. Any violation of this rule may result in round forfeits, map forfeits or disqualification at the discretion of the tournament administration.

**Player Disconnects:** If a single player disconnects or has a hardware failure, the team can request a technical pause. If the disconnect occurs very early in a round, before any significant action, an admin may decide to restart that round once the player reconnects. Before any significant action is defined as no damage dealt, no kills, and no engagement that reveals strategic information.

If the round was already underway, meaning any significant action occurred before the pause, the round usually continues with the remaining 4 players. Significant action includes damage, kills, or clear strategic information being revealed, such as utility usage or map control gained. The disconnected player may rejoin as soon as possible, but the round will not be replayed if such action occurred.

If multiple players on a team disconnect, or a major problem occurs mid-round such that the outcome of the round is heavily impacted or unclear, admins reserve the right to replay the round. If a technical issue is identified after a round is completed, for example a player who was lagging but did not disconnect, the round result stands unless the admins determine the issue severely affected fairness.

**Match Medics:** CS2's competitive mode includes a match restore feature. Admins will use match medic whenever possible to load the game state to what it was before the interruption. If match medic fails or is not possible for the exact state, the admin may restore the round score only and have teams play a normal start for the next round. In extreme cases a whole map restart could be considered, but only if a significant portion of the map was impacted and both teams agree or it is ruled necessary.

**No Replay for Misbuys or Team Error:** If a round is disrupted due to a team's own error, for example a player accidentally buying the wrong weapon or disconnecting due to alt-tabbing, the round will not be restarted. The game continues and the mistake stands. Admins will not consider this a valid reason to rewind a round.

# **Map Bugs and Glitches**

The intentional use of any game-breaking bugs, glitches or exploits is strictly prohibited. This includes, but is not limited to:

* Using any clipping bug to see or move through walls, floors, ceilings, or otherwise unintended areas of the map.

* Pixel walking, meaning standing on invisible map edges or pixels that allow hovering or an unintended vantage point.

* Any boosts or stacks that allow looking over or through solid areas of the map that designers did not intend to be peeked over. Boosting on teammates is generally allowed as long as it does not abuse a glitch; boosts that result in clipping your view through walls or skybox are illegal.

* Using scripts or macros to gain an unfair movement advantage, such as fast jump glitches beyond normal jumping techniques.

**Allowed Maneuvers:** Some in-game quirks are allowed if they are considered part of normal gameplay. Standing on a teammate's shoulders is allowed in general, except as noted above, and surfing on certain map elements is allowed if it does not involve a glitch. Shooting through walls or objects as allowed by game physics, and defusing through smokes or thin walls if the game permits it, are legal.

**Newly Discovered Bugs:** If a critical bug is discovered during the event, for instance a new one in a recent CS2 update, the admins will communicate a ruling on it. Teams are expected to check with admins if unsure about any trick or spot being legal. When in doubt, ask first. Using an exploit that is not explicitly listed but clearly goes against fair play or map design will still be penalised at the admins' discretion.

All participants are expected to compete with integrity and treat others with respect. The following guidelines are enforced.

**No Cheating:** Any form of cheating will result in immediate disqualification and potential bans. Cheating includes use of any external hacks, scripts or modifications that give an unfair advantage, such as aimbot, wallhack, trigger bots, no-flash or radar hacks. The tournament will have admins observing matches and VAC-enabled servers to detect cheats. If a player is caught cheating they will be banned from this event and potentially future events, with a standard ban period of two years for serious offences. Entire teams may be disqualified depending on the situation.

**No Ghosting:** Because this is an in-person LAN, ghosting, meaning receiving outside information about the game in progress, is strictly prohibited. Spectators and eliminated players must not communicate with players in an ongoing match. Coaches are only permitted to communicate as allowed. Any attempt by an outside party to relay information to players will lead to ejection of that spectator and potential penalties for the team. Where a live audience is present, the audience area is physically separated from the player area and additional ghosting controls, including delayed broadcast where applicable, are enforced.

**No Exploiting Game Bugs:** As covered in Map Bugs and Glitches, deliberately abusing a known glitch or bug is considered cheating and will be penalised. Minor unintended behaviours will be judged by the admins, and if we determine a team gained a round-winning advantage from an exploit, the round or match may be forfeited to the opponent or rematched, and warnings or disqualifications issued as appropriate.

**Forbidden Scripts and Configs:** Players may only use config settings and binds that are allowed in competitive play. Simple jump-throw binds, buy binds and standard alias commands are allowed. Illegal scripts such as recoil scripts and rapid-fire macros are forbidden. Any config file found to have forbidden commands, even if not used, can lead to a penalty, so players should double-check their configs. Software-based macros through mouse or keyboard drivers that replicate disallowed scripts are also banned.

**In-Game Communication:** Players should refrain from all chat during matches except for necessary communications, such as a polite gl hf or calling a technical timeout. Abuse of all chat, especially to taunt or complain, can lead to penalties. Harassment, extreme profanity or discriminatory language in chat or voice is not tolerated.

**Player Behaviour:** Sportsmanlike conduct is expected at all times. This means no harassment, bullying or unsportsmanlike behaviour toward opponents, teammates, staff, spectators or members of any live audience. This includes insults, slurs or any form of toxic behaviour. Any incident of serious unsportsmanlike conduct, such as throwing equipment or using hate speech, may result in a round penalty, map forfeit or disqualification depending on severity, and possibly removal from the venue. The tournament wants a friendly, respectful atmosphere, so celebrate victories humbly and accept losses gracefully.

**Compliance with Officials:** Teams must obey instructions from tournament admins and referees promptly. This includes pausing when told, restarting rounds when directed, or any administrative decisions. Arguing excessively with or disrespecting an official can itself result in penalties. If you have a disagreement or protest, captains should calmly raise it to admins at an appropriate time, not mid-round. The attempt to mislead or deceive admins with false information is considered misconduct and will be sanctioned.

# **Rule Enforcement and Penalties**

**Admin Decisions:** The head admin has final say on all matters of rule enforcement, match results and disputes. We will strive to be fair and consistent, referencing this rulebook. In unforeseen circumstances, admins will use their best judgment to uphold competitive integrity and sportsmanship. By participating, teams agree that the admins' decisions are final.

**Penalties:** If a rule is broken, possible penalties include but are not limited to warnings, loss of map, forfeiture of a match, disqualification from the tournament, and future bans. The penalty will fit the offence. Minor first-time offences might receive a warning, while clear malicious cheating is an immediate disqualification and ban.

**Technical Forfeits:** A team may forfeit a map or match if they are unable or unwilling to continue, or if a serious rule violation forces a forfeit. Not having five players ready to play will be treated as a forfeit loss for that map or match. A forfeited map in a Best-of-3 is recorded as a 0-1 loss, or 0-2 if forfeiting an entire match. The opponent either gets a bye or a default win.

**Protests:** If a team believes a rule was violated by the opponent or that there was an unfair situation, they should notify an admin immediately, ideally by pausing the game or during half-time or the end of a map. Do not wait until after the match to raise an issue that was evident during play. The admins will review evidence such as demos and logs if needed and make a ruling. Judgment calls by referees are not grounds for lengthy debate, and the admin decision is final after a protest.

**Integrity of Results:** All match results are considered official as reported by the server and admins. Any attempt to fake a match result, collude with another team to manipulate results, or intentionally lose is strictly forbidden. Such behaviour undermines the competition's integrity and will result in immediate disqualification and potential reporting to Valve, which could carry severe competitive bans.

**Betting:** Players, coaches and team staff are not allowed to bet on matches in this tournament. Any association with betting fraud or gambling on the outcome will lead to removal from the event.
